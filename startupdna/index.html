Why we do this

What is Startup DNA?

The headlines tell glittering stories of unicorns and high-flying IPOs. Behind it all are extraordinary dreamers with exceptional drive. Reaching beyond the pitch decks and demos, these are the founder's stories.

We call it sDNA. The show launched in 2026 from Taipei and in its first season became an official media partner of InnoVEX at COMPUTEX, the largest startup showcase in Asia's hardware capital.

About the show

We admire startups. But we love founders. For their vision, their grit, their unshakable will.

They are the modern-day explorers of new markets. Are they born or are they made?
