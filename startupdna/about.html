About

The makingsof a founder.

Startup DNA is a founder-interview podcast from Taipei. We call it sDNA.

The show

Beyond the pitch deck

We admire startups. But we love founders. For their vision, their grit, their unshakable will.

They are the modern-day explorers of new markets. Are they born or are they made?

The headlines tell glittering stories of unicorns and high-flying IPOs. Behind it all are extraordinary dreamers with exceptional drive.

Reaching beyond the pitch decks and demos, these are the founder's stories.

Each conversation runs 30 to 45 minutes and is recorded in Taipei, with shorter on-the-floor interviews filmed at events like InnoVEX. We publish on YouTube, with clips on Facebook and Instagram.

Host & Producer
Anthony Lin

Anthony Lin created Startup DNA after years observing the startup industry from a media perspective. Based in Taipei, he launched the show in 2026 with a simple conviction: the glittering headlines about unicorns and IPOs miss the part that matters, the people. Startup DNA reaches beyond pitch decks and demos to ask founders how they think, what drives them, and whether they were born or made. In its first season the show became an official media partner of InnoVEX at COMPUTEX Taipei, where the team mic'd up founders from AI robotics to analog intelligence to autonomous maritime systems.

How an episode happens

From intro to upload

The pitch

A founder, investor or operator reaches out, or we find them on a show floor. We look for a real story, not a product launch.

The conversation

Recorded in Taipei at The Studio. We ask about the origin, the hard turns, the people, and what the founder believes that others don't.

The story

Edited into a full episode plus clips. Then it lives here, with show notes, a founder profile, and the facts behind the headlines.
