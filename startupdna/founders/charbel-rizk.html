The founder

Who is Charbel Rizk?

Dr. Charbel Rizk is the founder and CTO of Oculi and the core inventor of its technology. Shortly after joining the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory in 2001, he was selected for the Missile Defense Agency's Innovation Cell, and in 2002 wrote a white paper proposing that machine vision should start at the pixel rather than at the image. Over the next two decades, as Associate Research Professor at Johns Hopkins and in senior roles at APL, more than US$40 million in funded research built the technology base that Oculi now holds exclusive worldwide rights to. An early pioneer in UAVs, AI and autonomous systems, Charbel came to InnoVEX 2026 looking for partners in Taiwan's semiconductor ecosystem.

The company

Oculi

Oculi is a fabless Johns Hopkins spinout commercialising the OCULI SPU, a Sensing and Processing Unit that runs AI directly at the pixel and transmits only the information a system needs to act on. The same software-defined chip can output full video or a minimal stream of "actionable information" and runs on milliwatts for most applications, cutting downstream processing by hundreds of times. In July 2026 Oculi closed the first tranche of a US$6 million Series Seed Plus led by Mosaic Fund with Kyocera CVC and GlobalFoundries, with whom it is co-developing the architecture.

Cameras are just digital mirrors. What would true machine vision look like?

  • Vision vs. imaging: why cameras are just digital mirrors and how Oculi's technology mimics the human eye for true visual intelligence.
  • The Oculi chip: a smart, programmable CMOS vision sensor that processes relevant data efficiently, saving energy and money.
