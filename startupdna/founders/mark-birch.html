The founder

Who is Mark Birch?

Mark Birch is the founder and CEO of TribeROI and the author of Community-in-a-Box, now in its second edition. His path runs through an enterprise HR startup he founded, Stack Overflow, where he helped launch and commercialize the enterprise business and expand it across APAC, and Amazon Web Services, where as Global Startup Advocate he ran the Founders in the Cloud newsletter and the AWS Startups Show, interviewing founders from seed to IPO. Along the way he built the Enterprise Sales Forum into a 30,000-member community across 24 chapters. In September 2024 Mark received the Taiwan Employment Gold Card, left AWS the following month, and chose Taipei over 30 other countries he'd visited as the place to launch TribeROI, citing affordability, runway, connectedness, culture and impact.

I boiled down why I chose Taiwan to five reasons: affordability & livability, runway, connectedness, culture, and impact.
Mark Birch · Founders in the Cloud

The company

TribeROI

TribeROI is an AI startup founded in Taipei in 2025 that helps businesses measure the health and business impact of their communities. It reads the tools a company already uses, without asking anyone to migrate, and connects engagement signals to finance data so that community leaders can show a dollar figure for the value they create. The company applies agentic AI to turn community engagement into measurable outcomes, from sales growth to innovation.

Community avoids the trap of influencers and AI bots by being a place where real people gather.
Mark Birch · Founders in the Cloud
