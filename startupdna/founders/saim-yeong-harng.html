The founder

Who is Saim Yeong Harng?

Saim Yeong Harng, known as Sam, is co-founder and CEO of Synvo AI, a deep-tech spinout from the Multimedia Laboratory (MMLab) at Nanyang Technological University in Singapore. His thesis is that today's AI can answer, generate and automate but lacks the institutional memory that lets an experienced employee build context over time. Synvo's answer is an Enterprise Memory Layer that lets AI agents retain, retrieve and reason across an organisation's documents, emails, video, audio and business data, designed from the start for on-premises and on-device deployment so sensitive information never leaves the company's control. In 2026 the company raised US$1 million to commercialise the technology across Asia, and Sam spoke on the InnoVEX Pi Stage, pitching Taiwan's mid-sized manufacturers and large corporates as a strategic gateway.

By giving AI an institutional memory, it accumulates value the longer a company uses it.
Saim Yeong Harng · Synvo AI funding announcement, 2026

The company

Synvo AI

Synvo AI builds a multimodal intelligence and memory layer for enterprise AI. Its commercial architecture, LiteMMem, is informed by FileGramOS, research with NTU's S-Lab that models procedural, semantic and episodic memory from how people actually create, edit and organise files; it posted the highest reported score on the FileGramBench benchmark. Deployed locally or on-device, Synvo compresses fragmented data into persistent context so that past decisions, corrections and customer exceptions carry forward, starting with knowledge-heavy workflows such as industrial quotations.
