The founder

Who is Lionel Chin?

Lionel Chin is Chief Operating Officer of Amity Robotics, where he runs end-to-end operations: product deployment, customer success, supply chain and partnerships across Southeast Asia and beyond. He describes himself as an intrapreneur, having spent two decades building and scaling products inside the Amity group, including as VP and Head of Engineering at Eko, SVP of Product and Engineering at Amity, and Managing Director of Amity Solutions, before taking the robotics business from a standing start. On Startup DNA he explains how the team deployed to nine countries and won clients like IHG and Southeast Asia's biggest malls in 16 months, why accents and dialects were the hardest technical problem, and why Taiwan is the next pilot market.

You configure the system around what you think guests will ask. Then they surprise you every time.
Lionel Chin · LinkedIn, 2026

The company

Amity Robotics

Amity Robotics, founded in Bangkok in 2024, builds ARC Base, a stationary multilingual AI concierge for hotels, malls and other high-traffic venues, powered by conversational AI and a proprietary Robotic Operating System. It is live in more than 30 properties across Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Hong Kong, the UAE and Saudi Arabia, for operators including IHG, Accor, Shangri-La, Pavilion Kuala Lumpur and Siam Piwat. At Crowne Plaza Changi Airport, the concierge "Clara" handles 26,000+ queries a year. In 2026 the company raised US$7 million and is preparing ARC Move, its first mobile robot.

Amity Robotics: Humanizing AI Robots

InnoVEX 2026 · 10:31 · Jun 5, 2026

More and more we'll find ourselves talking to robots at hotels, airports and shopping malls, even hospitals.

  • AI-powered customer experience: creating an interactive experience with non-humanoid autonomous concierge robots.
