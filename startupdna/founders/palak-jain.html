The founder

Who is Palak Jain?

Dr. Palak Jain is co-founder and CEO of Vellex Computing, a Stanford-spinout analog semiconductor company. She began her career as a power plant engineer in India, earned her PhD at the National University of Singapore, and went on to research roles at GE Global Research, Xilinx Research Labs and UC Berkeley before becoming a Cyclotron Road and Activate Fellow at Lawrence Berkeley National Lab. With co-founder Jason Poon, a Cal Poly professor and former Stanford postdoc, she is reviving analog computing to attack the cost and energy burden of modern AI training. At InnoVEX 2026, Vellex won both the IC Taiwan Grand Challenge and the Plug and Play Taiwan Pitch Contest; her story spans India, Singapore and Stanford.

Intelligence isn't fixed at the point of deployment. It grows with experience, adapts to its environment, and improves with every hour of operation.
Dr. Palak Jain · Vellex Computing

The company

Vellex Computing

Vellex Computing designs physics-based analog chips that train and adapt AI models directly on devices at milliwatt power, replacing brute-force cloud compute with the physics of the circuit itself. Founded in 2023 and headquartered in San Francisco, the company is backed by the U.S. National Science Foundation and Department of Energy, including a US$305,000 NSF SBIR Phase I grant in 2025, and targets edge AI in industrial IoT, robotics, humanoids and remote sensing, so that a satellite or drone six months into service is meaningfully better at its job than the day it shipped.
