The founder

Who is Daniel Yu?

Daniel Yu is co-founder and CEO of MetAI, the Taipei startup he launched in 2023 with co-founders Renton Hsu and Dave Liu to tear down the biggest barrier to physical AI: the months or years it takes to build an accurate digital twin. MetAI's generative model turns CAD files and 2D floor plans into simulation-ready 3D worlds in minutes, then produces synthetic data inside them to train robotic arms, humanoids and lights-out factories. In January 2025 the company closed a US$4 million seed round that made it the first Taiwanese company in NVIDIA's portfolio, alongside Kenmec, Solomon Technology, SparkLabs Taiwan, Addin Ventures and Upstream Ventures. Daniel has since grown the team almost tenfold in a year and is pushing MetAI toward a US presence, while urging Taiwanese founders to learn from American peers about storytelling and specialization.

Digital twins have long been seen as a barrier to entry for physical AI due to the months or even years of effort required for development.
Daniel Yu · TechCrunch, 2025

The company

MetAI

MetAI builds AI-powered, physics-based digital twins for semiconductor fabs, smart warehouses and automation. A member of NVIDIA Inception, it integrates NVIDIA Omniverse to generate "SimReady" environments instantly and to synthesize training data tailored to a customer's operations. Instead of isolated datasets, MetAI creates dynamic virtual worlds that behave like the real one, so that robots and autonomous systems can be trained and validated at scale before deployment.

Instead of creating isolated datasets, MetAI builds dynamic virtual worlds, realistic virtual environments that operate exactly like the real world.
Daniel Yu · TechCrunch, 2025
