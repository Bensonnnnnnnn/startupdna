The founder

Who is Ryan Yang?

Ryan Yang is Chairman of Tech Sphere Investments (TSI) and the lead investor and board member of Lean Mobility, the Japan-Taiwan micro-EV startup founded in 2022 by former Toyota Chief Engineer Akihiro Yanaka. Yanaka planned and developed the TOYOTA i-ROAD and led the C+pod and C+walk compact mobility programs before leaving to pursue the idea on his own; Ryan saw roughly 20 years of accumulated technology and core patents behind it. TSI led the company's JPY 450 million Pre-A round in 2025 and followed on in a 2026 extension that brought in Japan's public-private JICN climate fund, taking total funding to JPY 800 million. On Startup DNA he explains how Japanese engineering, Taiwanese angel capital and Tainan manufacturing come together, and why Europe's micromobility market is on the roadmap from day one.

Lean Mobility possesses unique dynamic control technology. We expect it to become a core player in next-generation MaaS and Robotaxi services.

The company

Lean Mobility

Lean Mobility's Lean3 is a two-seat, three-wheeled urban EV with air conditioning, a 100 km range, an 80 km/h top speed and a proprietary Active Lean System that tilts the body through corners. At 2,470 mm by 970 mm it is about a third the size of a passenger car and charges from a household outlet. Corporate pre-orders in Taiwan passed 500 units on day one in 2025; a mass-production plant is being built in Tainan ahead of deliveries in Japan and Taiwan from August 2026, with the LeanX data platform laying groundwork for future robotaxi services.
