The founder

Who is John Fan?

John Fan is the Taiwanese-American co-founder and CEO of PicCollage (Cardinal Blue Software), the Taipei-based photo and video app with more than 200 million downloads. He holds degrees from Princeton and a PhD in Electrical Engineering from Stanford, and previously worked at wireless startup Flarion, which was acquired by Qualcomm. After moving to Taipei in 2008, he and co-founder Jaime Cham spent years experimenting with Facebook-platform apps before a one-month prototype built for the first iPad went viral in 2011: 3,000 downloads on day one and 400,000 in the first month. Fifteen years on, John is known in Taiwan's ecosystem for a deliberately informal, demo-driven culture, a diverse international team, and for giving back, most recently through PicCollage's Day 1 Coworking Space in Taipei.

PicCollage now has 150 million users because we have constantly added new functions while keeping it easy to use.
John Fan · Taipei Times, 2017

The company

PicCollage

PicCollage is a photo and video collage app launched in summer 2011 and developed in Taipei. An alumnus of 500 Startups' second batch, it grew into a top-ten download in the US, UK, Canada and Mexico, reached 150 million users by 2017, and passed 250 million downloads in 2023. Rather than chase further venture rounds, the company moved to a sustainable subscription model and now ships new creative tools weekly, folding AI in as "magic effects" instead of marketing it as AI. PicCollage sponsors Startup DNA.

The environment in Taiwan is supportive for building creative things.
John Fan · Startup Island TAIWAN podcast, 2023

The episode

The App Powerhouse That Runs Like It's Day One

Founder Stories · 39:48 · Oct 15, 2026

How do you build a global app business on your own terms?

  • Accidental PMF: catching viral traction by solving social media's early single-photo limitation.
  • Stepping off the VC treadmill: pivoting from external funding to a sustainable subscription model.
  • Culture by design: using rituals like "lightning demos" and global diversity to keep 100+ team members agile and creative.
  • AI with less "AI": offering new tech as "magic effects" to enhance user creativity without the hype.
