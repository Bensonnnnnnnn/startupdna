The founder

Who is Caleb Chu?

Caleb Chu is the founding partner of Nova Studio, a Gen Z venture fund in Taipei that writes first checks to founders who are still students or fresh graduates, and the Chief Talent Officer at StarWorks Incubator, a venture studio that embeds its own operations, finance and go-to-market teams inside early-stage companies. A self-described serial dropout who became an entrepreneur at 16, Caleb carries the "battle scars" of a first startup that didn't work out and has turned that experience into a hands-on model for building alongside young founders. In 2026 he co-organised the inaugural Boba Tech Summit in Taipei with Stanford ASES, bringing roughly 300 Gen Z founders from 15+ countries together with speakers like Phison founder K.S. Pua, YouTube co-founder Steve Chen and Twitch co-founder Kevin Lin. His long game is to reinvent how Asia educates its builders.

Our goal is to become the first believer in young and ambitious founders before the path is clear, and help them scale from Asia to the world.
Caleb Chu · Nova Studio

The company

Nova Studio

Nova Studio describes itself as the "first believer" in exceptional Gen Z talent in Taiwan, investing US$15K to US$100K at pre-seed and seed, then opening doors across Taiwan, the US, Japan and Singapore. Founded in 2024, it has backed seven early-stage companies since 2025, several of which have gone on to programs such as Y Combinator, Pear, Plug and Play and Seedcamp. The fund doubles as a training ground: student investors learn deal sourcing, due diligence and valuation by doing it.
