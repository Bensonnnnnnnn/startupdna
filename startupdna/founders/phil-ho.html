The founder

Who is Phil Ho?

Phil Ho is co-founder and CEO of STR8 Industries, the US-Taiwan maritime autonomy company he started in 2025 with retired U.S. Navy officer Bilal Awad after a chance meeting over drinks. A veteran hardware venture builder, Phil chose to anchor the company in Taiwan, with a design studio in Hsinchu and a shipyard in Kaohsiung, because the island combines shipbuilding, electronics, computing, sensors and the engineering talent to integrate all of it. His vision for STR8's vessels is a mesh network rather than one operator per boat: every vessel is a mothership and none of them is, each running small tasks while collectively completing a larger mission. STR8 emerged from stealth at InnoVEX 2026, where it won an IC Taiwan Grand Challenge award, and joined AmCham Taiwan the same month.

The company

STR8 Industries

STR8 Industries builds autonomous unmanned surface vessels and the edge-native autonomy software that runs them. Its modular C-100M platform, publicly revealed as Ghostwake, is designed for rapid deployment and scalable production in Taiwan and has reached 55 knots in sea trials, with autonomous navigation, escort, patrol and search-and-rescue modes. The company's core technology is distributed edge-AI architecture: real-time fusion of radar, EO/IR, AIS and inertial sensors with low-latency decision frameworks for operations in extreme environments.
