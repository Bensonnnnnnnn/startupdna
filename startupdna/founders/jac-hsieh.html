The founder

Who is Jac Hsieh?

Jac Hsieh is the founder and CEO of NunoX, and the guest on Startup DNA's debut episode. A software developer by training with more than 20 years in product innovation, including time at Acer and in Silicon Valley, he previously founded DMKTZ and TG3D Studio, where he built 3D body-scanning tools for apparel. His grandparents were tailors; he remembers being carried on his grandmother's back while she sewed cheongsams one customer at a time, each taking a week. That memory became the mission: use technology to let garment makers grow. NunoX now partners with Taiwan's Textile Research Institute on an AI fabric-physics model, won a 2023 Edison Gold Award, joined NVIDIA Inception, and in October 2024 was qualified by Under Armour as a digital material supplier for its footwear supply chain.

I still remember that when I was a child my grandmother just carried me on her back and made clothes for her customers. I want to help them to grow their business.
Jac Hsieh · Startup DNA, "Scaling His Grandma's Dream"

The company

NunoX

NunoX turns physical fabric swatches into precise 1:1 digital twins. Its NX Premium Scanner captures a swatch in seconds, the NX Showroom cloud platform lets global teams simulate drape, collaborate and close deals on digital materials, and NX Muse organises scattered corporate data into an AI knowledge base. Validated by Under Armour's global deployment, the platform cuts the dozens of physical samples that fashion development normally burns through, reducing waste and time-to-market across the textile supply chain.

We want every step, from material selection to sampling, development, and production, to be sustainable.
Jac Hsieh · Taiwan News / CNA
