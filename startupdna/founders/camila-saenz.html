The founder

Who is Camila Sáenz?

Camila Sáenz is the founder of TUTEEMI, a Taipei platform that connects foreign language tutors with Taiwanese students. Born in Guatemala, she left home after high school, spent six months in China, and arrived in Taiwan on a government scholarship to study journalism at Ming Chuan University. She went on to complete an MBA and a PhD in business at National Taipei University of Technology, and has spoken at three TEDx events as well as at Google and Amazon. A popular English and Spanish tutor herself, she saw that tutors lacked stable income and parents lacked a trustworthy way to find them, so in 2019 she launched a Wix MVP that drew 50 tutor applications on day one. When Covid pushed education online, she doubled down on in-person mentorship. Since filming, Camila has also become a venture partner in Unicorn Tutor and runs Outliers Guatemala to bring Taiwan's startup lessons home.

The first day, we got over 50 people applying to be a tutor on our platform.
Camila Sáenz · Taiwan News

The company

TUTEEMI

TUTEEMI (originally Key Tutors) is a marketplace for discovering, scheduling and paying for one-on-one language lessons with foreign tutors living in Taiwan, in person or online. A 2019 Dragons' Chamber finalist, the company went through both the AppWorks and 500 Global (Taiwan Tech Arena) accelerators, acquired Private Tutor Taiwan in 2023, and now hosts well over 100 tutors serving students in Taiwan and abroad, including Guatemala, Japan, South Korea and Vietnam. Its pitch is not test prep but confidence, curiosity and a global mindset.

Actually I was not familiar with Taiwan, but I thought going to Taiwan would immerse me in a culture I had never experienced before.
Camila Sáenz · Taiwan News
